A simple website. A clear scope.

This page has no account registration, analytics, or advertising trackers. This notice covers this website. For information about a specific Wondrous Studio game, check the notice linked in that game or contact us.

01 About this notice

This notice explains privacy considerations when you visit the Wondrous Studio website or contact us by email. It covers this website and contact enquiries. It does not describe the data practices of a particular game.

For privacy information about a specific Wondrous Studio game, check the notice linked in that game or contact vtmytien240901@gmail.com.

02 Using this website

This website does not ask you to create an account or enter personal information into a form. The page itself does not set cookies, run analytics, display advertisements, or load third-party tracking pixels. Its navigation and visual interactions run in your browser.

When the website is hosted online, the hosting service may process technical request information, such as an IP address, browser information, the requested page, and the time of access. The information recorded and how long it is kept depend on the hosting configuration.

03 Contacting us by email

Choosing an email link opens your email application. A message is sent only if you choose to send it.

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any name, attachments, or other information you include. This information is used to understand and respond to your enquiry and manage related correspondence.

Please include only information relevant to your request. Do not send passwords, complete payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

04 Service providers and links

Email messages are handled by the email services used by you and Wondrous Studio. Website hosting services may also process technical information needed to deliver the website and operate their infrastructure.

These services have their own privacy practices. If you follow a link to another website or service, review its privacy notice before providing information there. This page does not control the content, settings, or data practices of those services.

05 Keeping information

Email correspondence may remain in the studio’s email system while an enquiry and any related follow-up are being handled. Retention can also depend on recordkeeping needs and applicable legal obligations.

You can contact us to ask about information you have sent or request its deletion. Information held by service providers, including backups or technical logs, may be subject to their own retention arrangements.

06 Your choices and requests

You can browse this page without sending us an email or providing information through a form.

To ask about personal information you have shared, request a correction, or request deletion, email vtmytien240901@gmail.com. Describe your request and provide enough information to identify the relevant correspondence. Additional information may be needed to verify a request before disclosing or changing personal information.

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have other rights concerning personal information.

07 Information about children

This website has no account registration or features asking children to submit personal information.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to Wondrous Studio through the contact email, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed. A game’s age suitability and its data practices should be assessed using that game’s own information.

08 Updates and contact

This notice may be updated when the website or the information it provides changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
